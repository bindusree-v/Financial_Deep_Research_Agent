## Key Research Findings

* TCS has historically managed to generate higher profits relative to its revenue compared to Infosys.
* TCS is trading at about 20-21 times earnings, a 25% discount from its 10-year average of 27.
* Infosys is trading around 22, which is only slightly lower than its historical average.
* TCS posted higher revenue from operations and a stronger Return on Capital Employed (ROCE) compared to Infosys.
* Infosys delivered a strong quarterly performance, but its revenue growth rate has been slower compared to TCS.
* TCS maintains its margin fortress at 25%, while Infosys' cautious 1.5%-3.5% guidance signals that the road to FY27 is paved with AI-led deflation.
* New labour code implementation dented Q3 FY26 profits of top IT firms, including TCS, Infosys, and others.

## Sector Trends & Insights

* IT sector companies are increasingly adopting AI-led pricing strategies, which may lead to deflation and impact profit margins.
* The implementation of new labour codes and gig economy regulations may lead to increased labor costs and impact profit margins.
* IT sector companies are experiencing fluctuations in their financial performance due to various market and economic factors.
* The IT sector is expected to continue growing due to increasing demand for digital services and solutions.
* TCS and Infosys are expected to continue competing with each other in the IT sector, with TCS maintaining its margin fortress and Infosys focusing on improving its cash flows.

## Risk Factors

* Fluctuations in market and economic conditions may impact the financial performance of TCS and Infosys.
* The implementation of new labour codes and gig economy regulations may lead to increased labor costs and impact profit margins.
* The adoption of AI-led pricing strategies may lead to deflation and impact profit margins.
* The IT sector is highly competitive, and TCS and Infosys may face intense competition from other IT sector companies.

## Investment Outlook

Based on our research, we expect TCS to continue maintaining its margin fortress and delivering strong financial performance. Infosys, on the other hand, is expected to focus on improving its cash flows and delivering strong quarterly performance.
