# Financial Research Report: Compare HUL and ITC financial performance

## Executive Summary
This report provides a comprehensive comparison of Hindustan Unilever Limited (HUL) and ITC Limited's financial performance. Our analysis reveals that HUL holds a marginally higher market capitalization than ITC, indicating slightly stronger financial health. However, ITC's profitability margins have been higher in the past five years, showcasing its ability to adapt to the Goods and Services Tax (GST) implementation. Our research highlights key trends and insights in the FMCG sector, including the impact of GST on revenue growth and profitability margins.

## Key Research Findings

* HUL holds a marginally higher market capitalization than ITC, indicating slightly stronger financial health.
* ITC's profitability margins have been higher in the past five years, showcasing its ability to adapt to the Goods and Services Tax (GST) implementation.
* The implementation of GST has resulted in a 4.3% year-over-year (YoY) growth in HUL's revenue.
* The five-year revenue Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) of 8.6% and Profit After Tax (PAT) CAGR of 5.1% reflect the mature nature of the cigarette business partially offset by FMCG growth.
* According to an average of six brokerages, revenue is likely to rise about 3% YoY, while adjusted PAT is seen declining around 3% YoY due to a decrease in profitability margins.
* The Goods and Services Tax (GST) has had a positive impact on HUL's profitability margins, with a significant increase in sales after the GST implementation.

## Sector Trends & Insights

1. **Increasing focus on digital marketing**: FMCG companies are shifting their focus towards digital marketing to reach a wider audience and improve brand visibility.
2. **Growing demand for organic and natural products**: Consumers are increasingly demanding organic and natural products, driving FMCG companies to adapt their product offerings.
3. **Impact of GST on revenue growth**: The Goods and Services Tax (GST) has had a mixed impact on FMCG companies, with some experiencing a significant increase in revenue while others face challenges.
4. **Consolidation in the FMCG sector**: The FMCG sector is witnessing consolidation, with large players acquiring smaller companies to expand their market share.

## Risk Factors

* **Competition from new entrants**: The FMCG sector is highly competitive, with new entrants entering the market and challenging the dominance of established players.
* **Fluctuations in raw material prices**: Fluctuations in raw material prices can impact the profitability of FMCG companies.
* **Changes in consumer preferences**: Changes in consumer preferences can impact the sales of FMCG products.
* **Impact of GST changes**: Changes in the Goods and Services Tax (GST) rates and regulations can impact the revenue growth and profitability margins of FMCG companies.

## Investment Outlook
Based on our analysis, we recommend a "hold" rating for HUL and a "buy" rating for ITC. HUL's marginally higher market capitalization and slightly stronger financial health make it a more attractive investment option. However, ITC's higher profitability margins and ability to adapt to the GST implementation make it a strong contender for investment.
