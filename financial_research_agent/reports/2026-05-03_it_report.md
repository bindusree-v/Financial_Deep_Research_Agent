## Sector Trends & Insights

* **Growing demand for digital transformation**: The COVID-19 pandemic has accelerated the adoption of digital technologies, leading to an increased demand for IT services.
* **Rising importance of data protection**: The Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023, and its subsequent rules have emphasized the need for companies to prioritize data protection and privacy.
* **Increasing focus on sustainability**: IT companies are under pressure to adopt sustainable practices and reduce their carbon footprint.
* **Growing competition from new entrants**: The Indian IT sector is experiencing increased competition from new entrants, including startups and multinational companies.

## Regulatory Environment

* **Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), 2023**: This act regulates the processing of personal data and provides individuals with greater control over their data.
* **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Rules, 2025**: These rules provide guidelines for companies to comply with the DPDPA, 2023, and emphasize the need for data protection and privacy.
* **Enforcement of DPDP Rules, 2025**: The rules came into effect on 13 November 2025, and companies are expected to comply with the regulations.

## Risk Factors

* **Compliance risks**: Companies may face risks related to compliance with the DPDPA, 2023, and its subsequent rules.
* **Reputation risks**: Companies may face reputational risks if they fail to prioritize data protection and privacy.
* **Regulatory risks**: Changes in regulations may impact the business operations of IT companies.

## Investment Outlook

Based on our analysis, we recommend a long-term investment in both TCS and Infosys. The companies have a strong financial performance, and the Indian IT sector is expected to grow at a CAGR of 10-12% over the next 5 years. However, investors should be aware of the risks associated with compliance, reputation, and regulatory changes.
